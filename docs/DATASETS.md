The JSON files in `config/families/` are relocatable templates, not completed
download manifests. Supply the exact BAMs, assemblies, caller inputs and TRGT
version used in an experiment. Preflight creates a full file-checksum inventory.
Human sequencing files are not included in this repository.

Public entry points:

- Genome in a Bottle: https://www.nist.gov/programs-projects/genome-bottle
- Jasmine/Iris study: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-022-01753-3
- Study implementation: https://github.com/mkirsche/Jasmine
- HG002 study VCF: http://data.schatz-lab.org/jasmine/HG002Trio/DeNovoDetection/denovo.merged.vcf.gz

The previously examined HG002 VCF SHA256 is
`33a3671a04fa19143bd8097bbbb214cda5b9985e5e94c9b0f1528c51253f135a`.
Verify a new download before use. Its GRCh38 VCF positions must not be passed
directly to a CHM13 benchmark. The six study records include three ≥50 bp SVs
and three 30–49 bp insertions. The mapped comparison and allele reconciliation
are separate from the candidate-discovery input.

Regular-region study analysis uses chr1–22 and excludes full event interval
overlap with the chosen CHM13 mask. Record the mask checksum; it is not
interchangeable with an active-centromere-only annotation. The historical study
mask SHA256 is `4c32552274bcac33051775d57d5fda30c8772a4f62649112440c939a05c3ea0a`.

No scientific counts are regenerated by these templates until the actual
study inputs and mapping contracts are supplied. The synthetic CI example
tests software execution rather than biological sensitivity.
